The future process

From requirement to completion.

1. Practice sets the opportunityDate, hours, location, rate, payment terms, skills, systems, competencies and experience.
2. Suitable dental nurses are identifiedOnly nurses who align with relevant criteria and availability are considered.
3. Dental nurses review clear informationThe opportunity is accepted or declined freely.
4. Practice receives the ToothScout Match SummaryAlignment and gaps are shown for an informed decision.
5. Both parties decide whether to proceedToothScout facilitates the connection and workflow.
6. Shift completion and payment workflowCompletion is recorded and the selected payment terms are followed.
