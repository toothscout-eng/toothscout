ToothScout Match Summary

Not just availability. Suitability.

The summary is designed as a concise decision aid, not a guarantee. The practice sees how the proposed dental nurse aligns with the stated requirement and decides whether to proceed.

Verification statusGDC, identity, Right to Work, Enhanced DBS, qualifications and references.
Requirement alignmentExperience, skills, competencies, systems and additional training.
Practical fitAvailability, location, travel area and working preferences.
Transparent gapsAny requested requirements not fully met are highlighted for the practice's decision.
